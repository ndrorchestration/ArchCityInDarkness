# Project brief — Arch City in Darkness
**Phase:** concept + initial source foundation. **Canon status:** exploratory; no approved story or assets.

## Player promise
An atmospheric, character-driven, original vampire urban RPG in Columbus, Ohio. Play a nocturnal newcomer navigating a living city: survival pressures, loyalties, secrets, social reputation, investigations, and consequences. Prefer player agency and interpersonal drama to grinding.

## Pillars (proposed)
1. **Reactive conversations:** dialogue conditions reflect past choices and current relationships.
2. **Multiple approaches:** each central investigation can be solved by social, stealth, or conflict routes without a mandatory fight.
3. **Survival with consequences:** hunger and public exposure produce interesting tradeoffs, not random soft-locks.
4. **Original supernatural politics:** factions and NPCs with independent motivations; no copies of existing licensed clans or chronicle roles.
5. **A lived-in Columbus:** fictional venues, original district layouts, local architectural inspiration and realistic street atmosphere. Real places are geographic reference, not claims of involvement.

## Tone
Contemporary urban gothic, humane but unsettling; rain, late-night transit, riverfront, arts and nightlife, layered light and sound. Character choices matter more than shock value. No copying of an existing game's dialogue, plot beats, quests or audiovisual identity.

## Provisional audience and delivery
Single-player Windows PC; third-person camera first, optional first-person exploration later; controller + keyboard/mouse design targets. Unreal Engine 5.8.4 target installation; native build NOT verified.

## Questions deferred to discovery
Specific protagonist origins and identity; supernatural taxonomy; original factions and their history; exact year and district layout; combat versus social weighting; mature content and accessibility settings. None should be treated as settled canon.

## Non-goals for first slice
Full open world, multiplayer, full faction hierarchy, cinematic full-cast models, voiced story, city-wide traffic, real-world map-scale simulation, commercial release, imported copyrighted material.

## Source-of-truth policy
This public repo stores original game design and implementation only. Keep the user's Columbus in Darkness historical archive and any confidential player messages outside this repo. A factual design comparison to a known game is not authorization to reimplement protected expressive content.
