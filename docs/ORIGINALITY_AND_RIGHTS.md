# Originality, rights, and privacy guardrails
**Status:** working production constraints; not legal advice, legal clearance or a confirmed trademark search.

## Inspirations are not assets
A modern vampire RPG may use broad genres and gameplay patterns such as survival needs, persuasion checks, branching outcomes, stealth, faction reputation, and third-person exploration. Do not transplant expressive content from Vampire: The Masquerade — Bloodlines, World of Darkness, One World by Night, or the Columbus in Darkness LARP.

## Do not copy
- Existing character or clan identities, protected species mythology, specific rule text, setting lore, plot arcs, quest text and structure, dialogue, recognizable visual designs, voice lines, music, UI artwork, maps, textures, models, or game code.
- Content found in story transcripts, roleplay emails, wiki pages, Discord logs, licensed books, mods, or fan art merely because it is publicly accessible.
- Names or recognizable lightly altered versions of player characters or private participants without appropriate permissions.

## Independent creative process
Record original creative decisions in the project docs and preserve design provenance. Draw from Columbus's real geography and original field references, using fictional businesses and original characters. Label generated/proxy assets REVIEW ONLY until verified and approved. Review the story and art for substantial similarity before public release.

## Title and brand risk
`Arch City in Darkness` is a **working title only**. The real Columbus in Darkness vampire LARP and World of Darkness associations create potential name/market confusion despite differing punctuation or substitution. Separately, 'Arch City' is strongly associated with St. Louis; assess geographic clarity and market confusion. Check relevant trademark databases, store listings, and rights counsel before promotional branding.

## Public repository and contributor safeguards
Repository is public. Do not commit confidential IC/OOC player mail, personally identifying participant information, real-life private communications, purchased/third-party assets without their licenses, API tokens, or private research files. No outside license or rights grant has been established for this new project.

## Review gate
Before a public demo or monetization: originality audit for narrative, gameplay naming, visuals and audio; trademark/title clearance; verify any third-party asset licenses; privacy review.
