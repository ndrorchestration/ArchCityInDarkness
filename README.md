# Arch City in Darkness

**Status: Incubating game-development project — no independently verified playable release.**

Arch City in Darkness is an original urban-supernatural game project set in Columbus, Ohio. The near-term creative goal is a focused **prelude and introductory vertical slice**, establishing the city atmosphere, player onboarding and a first meaningful gameplay encounter. This describes project direction, not completed implementation.

## Planned first-slice pillars

- **Setting and narrative:** Ground the fiction in a recognizable Columbus-inspired cityscape while keeping story, characters and art original.
- **Prelude:** Establish setting, mood and central playable premise through a short introductory sequence.
- **Player controls and interaction:** Create one controllable character, a traversable environment and at least one verifiable interaction.
- **Cinematic presentation:** Intentional camera framing, environmental lighting and coherent visual language, subject to native-engine verification.
- **Evidence and release:** Record exact source revisions, test results, capture provenance and open defects. A passing source-level check is not proof of a playable, approved release.

These pillars are **targets**, not statements that working assets or mechanics already exist. Engine/runtime selection, completed gameplay, rights clearance and release readiness must be verified from project-local evidence before they are claimed.
